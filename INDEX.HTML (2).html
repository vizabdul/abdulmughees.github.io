The Mind
Behind The Data.

I'm Abdul Mughees, a Data Analyst based in Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh, India — with a foundation in Life Sciences and Computer Applications that gives me a rare interdisciplinary lens for data problems.

My professional journey spans operational data analysis at DC Associates, virtual simulations with J.P. Morgan (quantitative research) and Deloitte Australia (data analytics consulting), along with a growing portfolio of self-driven analytics projects.

I specialize in transforming messy, complex datasets into clear visualizations and actionable business insights — bridging the gap between raw numbers and real-world decisions.

"My mission is to make data speak in a language that drives action — because the value of analysis is always in the decision it enables."

I'm actively targeting data and business intelligence roles in Gulf markets, including Saudi Arabia, and am open to relocation for the right opportunity.
